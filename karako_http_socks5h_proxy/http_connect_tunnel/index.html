CONNECT handling and the raw byte tunnel behind it.

A successful CONNECT has two parts. First the proxy opens a SOCKS5 connection to the requested authority and answers 200 OK; hyper then upgrades the client connection and hands it back. Second, the upgraded stream and the SOCKS5 socket are spliced together and copied in both directions until one side closes or the idle timeout fires.
