Module response_header_policy 

Source
Expand description

Optional rewriting of response headers, plus two synthetic policy files.

Three independent switches act on every response the proxy emits:

  • --boostaler-cache extends Cache-Control with stale-serving directives.
  • --cors-allow-all adds permissive CORS headers and serves mocks for the two legacy cross-domain policy files.
  • --strip-security-headers removes CSP and related security headers.

Only the response head is rewritten, except for the policy mocks, which replace the body as well. Nothing here can reach inside a CONNECT tunnel: after the proxy answers 200, the remaining bytes are the client’s encrypted session.
